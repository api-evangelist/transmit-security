The article examines escalating cybersecurity threats in 2026, particularly those enabled by generative AI, arguing that fraudsters now easily bypass account-opening controls through deepfakes and synthetic identity tactics while "Five Eyes" cyber chiefs warn AI-driven major cyberattacks are months away. It recommends financial institutions adopt a full customer-lifecycle security strategy, layered authentication replacing legacy systems, and combined fraud/scam prevention through staff training and money mule detection.
